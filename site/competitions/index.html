Science 11

  • S

    Science Olympiad

    Team event with 20+ science & engineering events (study, build and lab); regionals → states → nationals. The most beginner-friendly.

    Grades 6-12Team
    Varies
  • S

    Science Bowl

    Fast buzzer quiz across physics, chem, bio, earth science and math; regional winners advance to nationals.

    Grades 9-12Team
    Varies
  • U

    USA Biolympiad (USABO)

    Hard biology exam; top scorers reach semifinals and a national training camp.

    Grades 9-12Solo
    Varies
  • C

    Chemistry Olympiad (USNCO)

    Chemistry exam series: local → national → two-week study camp.

    Grades 9-12Solo
    Varies
  • P

    Physics Olympiad (F=ma)

    The F=ma exam qualifies you for the USAPhO and the national physics camp.

    Grades 9-12Solo
    Varies
  • B

    Brain Bee

    Neuroscience competition based on the free Brain Facts book; winners can earn research placements.

    Grades 9-12Solo
    Varies
  • I

    iGEM

    Build a synthetic-biology project end to end; the premier synbio competition (usually needs lab access or a mentor).

    Grades 9-12Team
    Varies
  • U

    USA Astronomy & Astrophysics Olympiad (USAAAO)

    A 75-minute first-round exam each February, proctored by a teacher at your school ($30, aid available); top scorers move on toward the international astronomy olympiad.

    Grades 9-12Solo
    Varies
  • N

    National Ocean Sciences Bowl (NOSB)

    Buzzer quiz on the biology, chemistry, geology and physics of the oceans; regional bowl winners go to the national finals.

    Grades 9-12Team
    Varies
  • N

    NCF-Envirothon

    Hands-on environmental science contest (soils, aquatic ecology, forestry, wildlife) that runs from state events to an international final with $50,000+ in scholarships and prizes.

    Grades 9-12Team
    Varies
  • G

    Genes in Space

    Propose a DNA experiment to run in space, alone or with a partner and an adult sponsor; the winning idea flies to the International Space Station. No lab experience needed.

    Grades 7-12Solo or pair
    Apr

Math 7

  • A

    AMC → AIME → USAMO

    The main US math olympiad ladder: AMC 10/12, then AIME, then USA(J)MO. Practice on Art of Problem Solving.

    Grades 9-12Solo
    November
  • M

    MathWorks Math Modeling (M3)

    Solve a real-world problem with mathematical modeling over one intense weekend.

    Grades 11-12Team
    Varies
  • U

    USA Mathematical Talent Search (USAMTS)

    Free proof-based contest: a month per round to write up five problems, with written feedback on your work. A path to the AIME, for US students who haven't finished high school.

    Grades 6-12Solo
    Varies
  • H

    HMMT (Harvard-MIT Math Tournament)

    One of the biggest high school math tournaments, held each November at Harvard and February at MIT. A coach registers teams in September ($80 per team).

    Grades 9-12Team
    Sep
  • A

    ARML (American Regions Math League)

    Regional teams compete each June at several university sites in team, power, individual and relay rounds. Ask your state or local math league how to try out.

    Grades 9-12Team
    Varies
  • M

    MATHCOUNTS Competition Series

    The main middle school math contest, from chapter to state to nationals. Schools register by mid-December ($42-$48 per student, half off for Title I schools); students whose school doesn't take part can enter on their own.

    Grades 6-8Team or solo
    Dec 15
  • P

    Purple Comet! Math Meet

    Free online team contest held over a ten-day window each spring: teams of up to six, with an adult supervisor, solve as many problems as they can in 60 or 90 minutes.

    Grades 6-12Team
    Apr

Computer science 7

  • U

    USA Computing Olympiad (USACO)

    Competitive programming contests with Bronze → Platinum divisions; top scorers reach the USACO camp.

    Grades 9-12Solo
    Varies
  • H

    Hackathons

    Build a project in 24-48 hours. Hack Club lists tons of free high-school hackathons.

    Grades 9-12Team
    Varies
  • P

    picoCTF

    Carnegie Mellon's free capture-the-flag hacking competition each March, with practice challenges all year; US middle and high schoolers can win prizes.

    Grades 13+Team or solo
    Mar
  • C

    CyberPatriot

    The Air Force Association's cyber defense competition: teams find and fix security holes in virtual computers. Registration usually closes in early October; JROTC, Civil Air Patrol and Sea Cadet teams pay no fee.

    Grades 6-12Team
    Varies
  • T

    Technovation Girls

    Build a mobile app or AI project that solves a problem in your community, then pitch it. The season runs October to May, and a co-ed track opens for 2027.

    Grades Ages 8-18Team
    Varies
  • N

    NASA Space Apps Challenge

    A free global hackathon each fall (Nov 14-15 in 2026) where teams of up to six use NASA data to tackle real challenges, at local events or online.

    Grades All agesTeam
    Nov 14
  • A

    American Computer Science League (ACSL)

    Four short contests a year on computer science fundamentals and programming, taken at your school, with divisions from elementary to AP level. A team costs $175.

    Grades 3-12School team
    Varies

Research 5

  • R

    Regeneron ISEF

    The largest international science fair; qualify through a regional or state fair, then present a project.

    Grades 9-12Solo or team
    Varies
  • R

    Regeneron Science Talent Search (STS)

    The most prestigious US research competition; judges your project and full academic profile. Start the essays early.

    Grades 12Solo
    November
  • J

    Junior Science & Humanities Symposium (JSHS)

    Present original STEM research at a regional symposium for scholarships and a shot at nationals.

    Grades 9-12Solo
    Varies
  • D

    Davidson Fellows

    $10k-$50k scholarships for significant projects in STEM, literature, music and beyond.

    Grades K-12 (under 18)Solo
    February
  • B

    Breakthrough Junior Challenge

    Explain a science or math concept in a short video for a large scholarship.

    Grades 13-18Solo
    June

Innovation 6

  • C

    Conrad Challenge

    Design an innovation or business to solve a real problem, then pitch it to judges.

    Grades 9-12Team
    November
  • D

    Diamond Challenge

    High-school entrepreneurship competition with a written concept round and a live pitch.

    Grades 9-12Team
    November
  • M

    MIT THINK Scholars

    Propose a STEM project (no prior research needed); winners get funding and MIT mentorship to build it.

    Grades 9-12Solo or team
    Varies
  • W

    Wharton Global High School Investment Competition

    Free: teams of four to six with a teacher advisor manage a virtual portfolio for a client from late September to early December. Advisors register teams in August and September.

    Grades 9-12Team
    Sep
  • S

    Samsung Solve for Tomorrow

    Public school classes propose a STEM project that helps their community, entered by a teacher. National winners get $100,000 prize packages of technology and classroom supplies.

    Grades 6-12Class team
    Nov 23
  • B

    Blue Ocean Student Entrepreneur Competition

    Free virtual pitch contest for high schoolers worldwide: take a short online course, then submit a five-minute video pitch. Top prizes are $1,000, $750 and $500, plus regional awards.

    Grades 9-12Solo or team
    Feb 21

Humanities 6

  • Q

    Quiz Bowl

    Buzzer trivia across every subject; question patterns repeat, so practice pays off fast.

    Grades 9-12Team
    Varies
  • A

    Academic Decathlon

    Ten-event academic competition with GPA-based divisions, built so everyone can compete.

    Grades 9-12Team
    Varies
  • N

    NACLO (Linguistics)

    North American Computational Linguistics Open - fun logic puzzles, no prior linguistics needed.

    Grades 9-12Solo
    January
  • S

    Scholastic Art & Writing Awards

    The most recognized creative arts & writing awards for teens; colleges know them.

    Grades 7-12Solo
    December
  • N

    National History Day

    Research a topic on the yearly theme (2027: Innovation in History) and present it as a paper, exhibit, documentary, website or performance, from local contests to nationals in June.

    Grades 6-12Solo or group
    Varies
  • J

    John Locke Institute Essay Competition

    Free global essay prize on big questions in philosophy, politics, economics, history and more, due in late spring. Winners get credit toward the Institute's summer programmes, not cash.

    Grades Under 19Solo
    Varies

Robotics 4

  • F

    FIRST Robotics (FRC / FTC)

    Build and code competition robots in large team events; grants exist to help cover costs.

    Grades 7-12Team
    Varies
  • S

    SeaPerch

    Build an underwater robot (ROV) from a kit and race it through obstacle courses at 100+ regional events; top teams earn spots at the International SeaPerch Challenge.

    Grades Elementary to high schoolTeam
    Varies
  • B

    BEST Robotics

    Free for schools: teams get a kit of everyday materials and eight weeks to build a robot for a game-day contest, plus a judged award for design and presentation.

    Grades 6-12School team
    Varies
  • V

    VEX Robotics

    Build smaller competition robots; fall season, widely accessible for new teams.

    Grades 9-12Team
    Varies
