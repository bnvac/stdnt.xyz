stdnt.xyz is a hand-curated, searchable directory of the free stuff students can get: free tools and API keys, student perks and discounts, scholarships, STEM programs, competitions and hackathons. Being a student (and honestly, just being online) unlocks thousands of dollars of free stuff - it's just scattered everywhere. This pulls the best of it into one place you can search, filter and save from.

How to read it

Student needs a school email (.edu) or a quick verification such as SheerID. Hack Club perks are for teens 13-18.

Everyone is free for anyone, no strings attached.

Tap the star on anything to save it to your list. It stays on this device.

How to use it

  • Search any tab from the box up top (or press /).
  • Filter Tools by category & access, Scholarships by type, Programs by grade.
  • Sort scholarships by amount, programs by top picks (Editor's choice first).
  • Watch the Deadlines tab and export them to your calendar.
  • Find live Hackathons, Guides and Templates under the More menu.
  • Star favourites, then open Saved at the top of the page.

How we pick

What gets listed

  • Free, or free for students. When a program does cost money, the price is shown up front.
  • Real, currently running and linked to the official page: no aggregators, referral or affiliate links.
  • No piracy, paywall bypasses or “shadow libraries”.

Editor's choice

A short list of standouts: the tools we'd tell a friend to grab first, and programs that are highly selective, strong on mentorship and usually free or funded. It's a judgment call, never a paid placement.

Where the data comes from

  • Community spreadsheets, contributor submissions and our own research, checked against official pages.
  • A Verified tag means a person reviewed that listing that month.
  • Every link is tested weekly. Broken ones show “Link may be down” until they're fixed.
  • Dates shift from year to year, so confirm deadlines on the official site.

Spot a problem?

Use the flag on any listing to report a dead link or a wrong detail, or suggest something new from the Contribute tab.
