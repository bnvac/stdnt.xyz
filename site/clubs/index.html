Clubs and volunteering for students

Start a chapter of a national club at your school, or volunteer in person or online. Rules and dues vary by state, so check each official page.

Career & business 8

  • D

    DECA

    Business, marketing, finance and hospitality club known for its role-play and case competitions, from districts up to an international conference.

    10 students + a teacher advisor

    High school & college$8 national dues + state dues
  • F

    FBLA (Future Business Leaders of America)

    Business competitions, leadership conferences and career prep, with state and national events every year.

    5 members + a teacher adviser

    Middle school to collegeDues vary by state
  • H

    HOSA (Future Health Professionals)

    For future doctors, nurses and other health professionals: health science competitions, leadership and service. Chapters affiliate through their state HOSA each year.

    A teacher advisor + your state HOSA

    Middle school to collegeDues vary by state
  • T

    TSA (Technology Student Association)

    STEM competitions from coding and robotics to engineering design and video production, with state conferences and a national one.

    A teacher advisor; members take a tech-related course

    Middle & high schoolFrom $12 a member + state dues
  • S

    SkillsUSA

    Career and technical education club for the trades, technology and health: hands-on skills competitions plus leadership training.

    A teacher advisor (classroom or virtual chapter)

    Middle school to collegeDues vary by state
  • B

    BPA (Business Professionals of America)

    Competitions and leadership in business, finance, IT, management and communication, with state and national conferences.

    A teacher advisor applies online

    Middle school to collegeDues vary by state
  • F

    FCCLA (Family, Career and Community Leaders of America)

    For culinary, fashion, early childhood, interior design and hospitality students: 50+ competitive events, service projects and leadership.

    A Family and Consumer Sciences teacher as adviser

    Middle & high schoolDues vary by state
  • J

    JA Company Program (Junior Achievement)

    Launch and run a real student business over a semester or a school year with volunteer mentors, then compete for JA Company of the Year.

    Your local Junior Achievement office, in school or after school

    High school

STEM & coding 6

  • H

    Hack Club

    Start a coding club at your school with up to $100 in starter funding, stickers, hardware and a big Slack community of teen hackers.

    Apply online to lead a club

    High schoolFree
  • G

    Girls Who Code Clubs

    Coding clubs that meet after school, on weekends or online. Ask a teacher, librarian or parent to apply as facilitator; they can learn alongside the club.

    An adult facilitator (no coding experience needed)

    Grades 3-12Free
  • S

    SWENext Clubs (Society of Women Engineers)

    The Society of Women Engineers' STEM clubs for students of all genders, with mentors from local SWE sections and 300+ clubs worldwide.

    Register a club of any size

    Ages 5-18Free
  • N

    NSBE Jr. (National Society of Black Engineers)

    Pre-college chapters of the National Society of Black Engineers: STEM projects, competitions and the Pre-College Initiative conference.

    5 paid members + an adult advisor

    Grades K-12$5 a year per member
  • S

    SHPE Jr. (Society of Hispanic Professional Engineers)

    High school chapters of the Society of Hispanic Professional Engineers, open to any student interested in science, technology, engineering or math.

    10 members + an advisor who works at your school

    High school
  • M

    Mu Alpha Theta

    The national math honor society: chapters run competitions and peer tutoring, and members can apply for its awards and scholarships.

    A math teacher sponsor; your school must offer advanced math

    High school & 2-year collegeChapter fee

Service 8

  • K

    Key Club

    The oldest and largest high school service club, run by students and sponsored by Kiwanis: local service projects, leadership and district conventions.

    A sponsor (usually Kiwanis), a faculty advisor + 11 members

    High schoolMember dues
  • I

    Interact (Rotary)

    Rotary's service clubs for teens: at least two projects a year, one for your community and one that promotes international understanding.

    A sponsoring Rotary club

    Ages 12-18Free to certify
  • L

    Leo Clubs (Lions)

    Lions Clubs' youth service clubs, based at a school or in the community: Alpha Leos for ages 12-18 and Omega Leos for 18-30.

    A sponsoring Lions club

    Ages 12-30
  • R

    Red Cross Clubs

    Official student clubs of the American Red Cross: run blood drives and disaster-preparedness events, with at least three service projects a year.

    3-6 founding members + your school's approval

    Middle school to college
  • B

    Best Buddies

    Student-run friendship clubs that pair students one-to-one with peers with intellectual and developmental disabilities.

    A teacher advisor

    Middle school to collegeNo startup fee
  • H

    Habitat for Humanity campus chapters

    Student-led chapters that build homes with local Habitat affiliates and raise awareness about affordable housing; colleges can also join Collegiate Challenge break trips.

    High school & college
  • C

    Circle K International

    Key Club's college counterpart: a student-run campus service and leadership club, sponsored by a local Kiwanis club.

    A Kiwanis sponsor, an advisor + 15 members

    CollegeMember dues
  • K

    Keystone Club (Boys & Girls Clubs)

    The Boys & Girls Clubs' teen leadership club: members elect officers and run service, career and community projects, with a national conference each year.

    A Boys & Girls Club and an adult advisor

    Ages 14-18

Causes & advocacy 6

  • U

    UNICEF Clubs

    Advocate for children's rights, educate your school and fundraise for UNICEF's work, as part of a national network of student clubs.

    4 student leaders + an adult advisor (25+)

    High school & college
  • G

    Girl Up

    The UN Foundation's clubs for gender equity: campaigns, fundraising and leadership training. Clubs are open to everyone, boys included.

    5 core members (+ an adult advisor if most are under 18)

    Middle school to college
  • A

    Amnesty International student groups

    Run human rights campaigns, letter-writing events and awareness drives alongside Amnesty International USA's 600+ youth and student groups.

    Register each year + a short online orientation

    High school & collegeFree
  • A

    Active Minds

    Peer-led mental health advocacy chapters that run awareness events and push their schools to take mental health seriously; 500+ chapters nationwide.

    3 students + an advisor

    High school & college
  • R

    Roots & Shoots (Jane Goodall Institute)

    The Jane Goodall Institute's youth groups: pick a problem in your community, for people, animals or the environment, and run a project to fix it.

    A group advisor

    All ages
  • O

    Operation Smile student clubs

    Raise money and awareness for free cleft surgeries, with training and support from Operation Smile's student programs team.

    A faculty advisor + your school's approval

    Elementary to college

Academic & debate 4

  • N

    National Speech & Debate Association (NSDA)

    Start a speech and debate team: tournaments, honor society recognition and the national tournament. Grants help new and Title I programs cover dues.

    A teacher advisor signs your school up

    Middle & high school$149/yr per school + $20/student
  • N

    National Honor Society (NHS)

    The best-known high school honor society, built on scholarship, service, leadership and character. Members can apply for the NHS Scholarship.

    Your school applies (not students): a principal plus a six-member faculty council

    High school$385/year, paid by the school
  • Q

    Quill and Scroll

    The international honor society for high school journalists: a lifetime charter for your school's student media, plus writing contests and scholarships.

    A school newspaper, yearbook, magazine, broadcast or website and its adviser

    High school journalistsNo annual dues; $25 once per member
  • T

    Tri-M Music Honor Society

    NAfME's music honor society: chapters recognize student musicians and run music service projects in their schools and communities.

    Any faculty member as chapter advisor

    Middle & high school$100/year per chapter

Volunteer online 12

  • S

    Schoolhouse.world

    Tutor other students in free small-group sessions over Zoom (founded by Sal Khan). Tutors build a verified portfolio recognized by 50+ colleges.

    Get certified in a subject first

    High school & upFree
  • U

    UPchieve

    Tutor and coach low-income middle and high schoolers online, on your own schedule. No tutoring experience needed.

    Free training; no minimum hours

    9th grade & up
  • L

    Learn To Be

    Free one-on-one online tutoring for underserved kids. Hours are verified and count toward the Presidential Volunteer Service Award.

    1-2 hour-long lessons a week for 4+ months

    High school & up
  • C

    Crisis Text Line

    Volunteer from home as a crisis counselor, texting with people who need support. Training is self-paced and shifts fit your schedule.

    Free 15-hour training, then 200 hours

    18+
  • B

    Be My Eyes

    Answer a video call when a blind or low-vision person needs a quick pair of sighted eyes, like reading a label. Help whenever you're free.

    The free app and a phone

    17+Free
  • Z

    Zooniverse

    Help real researchers by classifying galaxies, wildlife photos and historic documents from your browser.

    A browser, no experience needed

    All ages (under 16 with a parent's OK)Free
  • S

    Smithsonian Transcription Center

    Transcribe historic diaries, letters and scientific specimen records so the Smithsonian's collections become searchable.

    14+ to registerFree
  • M

    Missing Maps

    Trace buildings and roads from satellite images in OpenStreetMap so aid groups can reach people in disaster-prone places.

    Quick to learn, no experience needed

    Open to allFree
  • D

    Distributed Proofreaders

    Proofread scanned public-domain books page by page to turn them into free e-books on Project Gutenberg.

    No minimum, even a page a day

    Open to allFree
  • S

    SciStarter

    Search citizen science projects you can do online or near you, and track your contributions in one place.

    Open to allFree
  • I

    iNaturalist

    Photograph plants, animals and fungi; the community identifies them and your observations feed real biodiversity research.

    13+ (under 13 with a parent)Free
  • N

    NASA Citizen Science

    About 50 NASA projects anyone can join from a phone or laptop, from hunting for exoplanets to classifying galaxies; 500+ volunteers have co-authored papers.

    Open to allFree

Volunteer near you 7

  • D

    DoSomething

    Volunteer and civic action campaigns you can do on your own time. Finishing eligible ones enters you for scholarships, with no essay or GPA.

    Ages 13-25 (US)Free
  • I

    Idealist

    Search 100,000+ volunteer opportunities, on-site and virtual. VolunteerMatch merged into Idealist in 2025.

    Open to allFree
  • A

    American Red Cross youth volunteering

    Help at blood drives, pack comfort kits for disaster survivors or join a youth first aid team; all training is free. At 16 you can teach first aid and CPR.

    A parent's consent + a 6-month commitment

    Ages 13-17
  • V

    Volunteer.gov

    The US government's volunteer portal: find roles with national parks, forests, wildlife refuges and other federal sites.

    Varies by roleFree
  • S

    Special Olympics

    Coach, officiate, help at events or play alongside athletes with intellectual disabilities as a Unified partner through your local program.

    Varies by program
  • A

    Audubon Christmas Bird Count

    Join a one-day bird count in your area between December 14 and January 5, part of one of the longest-running community science projects.

    Email the organizer of a count circle near you

    Open to allFree
  • A

    AmeriCorps

    National service in tutoring, disaster response, conservation and more. Most programs pay a living allowance, and a full-time term earns an education award worth the maximum Pell Grant.

    A full- or part-time term of service

    Mostly 18+ (some programs 16-17)
