    LLCipher (Lincoln Laboratory cryptography workshop)

    Free one-week MIT Lincoln Laboratory course in theoretical cryptography at MIT Beaver Works in Cambridge: build a secure encryption scheme and digital signature. Non-residential, so plan your own housing and transport. For U.S. citizens and permanent residents.

  • L

    LLRISE (Lincoln Laboratory Radar Introduction for Student Engineers)

    Free two-week residential workshop at MIT and MIT Lincoln Laboratory where rising seniors build a Doppler and range radar alongside Lab engineers. U.S. citizens only; permanent residents aren't eligible.

    Program
    Added Sep 28

    MIT Lincoln Laboratory Summer Research Program (SRP)

    Paid summer research internships at MIT Lincoln Laboratory in Lexington, MA for college students who have finished sophomore year and for grad students. U.S. citizens only; travel help and discounted housing for students coming from 50+ miles away.
